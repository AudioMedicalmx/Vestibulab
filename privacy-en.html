VestibuLab privacy notice

Last updated: October 25, 2026 · Versión en español (the Spanish version is the legally binding one)

This notice explains what personal data VestibuLab collects, what we use it for and how you can exercise your rights, in accordance with Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties.

Data controller

Dr. Antonio Aldana Vargas (AudioMedical MX), Calzada México-Xochimilco 118, interior 107, colonia San Lorenzo Huipulco, alcaldía Tlalpan, C.P. 14370, Mexico City, Mexico. Contact: audiomedicalmx@gmail.com.

Data we collect

The first time you use VestibuLab, we ask you to complete a short registration with:

  • First name and last name(s).
  • Country.
  • Email address.
  • Profession or training stage.
  • Workplace or institution.
  • Main use of the app (study, clinical care, teaching, research or other).
  • Whether you agree to receive updates and course announcements.

We also store the registration date, the app version and whether you use it on the web or on Android. We do not collect sensitive data, patient data or health data. Simulations run only on your device and are not sent to any server.

Account and trial: to give you the 30-day full trial and, if you buy it, VestibuLab Pro on all your devices, we confirm your email with a one-time code. With your account we store your email, the trial start and end dates, whether you have VestibuLab Pro and, if applicable, the identifier and amount of your payment. If you delete your account we keep only an encrypted fingerprint of your email (a code from which it cannot be recovered) with your trial end date, so that the free trial does not restart, plus the payment records required by tax law.

What we use your data for

Necessary purposes:

  • Providing access to VestibuLab and keeping a record of its users.
  • Understanding users' professional profile and use of the app in order to improve it as an educational tool.
  • Producing general, anonymous statistics (for example, number of users by country or profession).
  • Contacting you about important changes to the app or to this notice.

Optional purpose: sending you updates, courses and academic activities from AudioMedical MX, only if you checked that box. You can withdraw this consent at any time by writing to us.

Where it is stored

Registration and account data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions), a service of Google LLC that acts as data processor and may host the information outside Mexico. Nobody can read it from the app: only the VestibuLab server and the data controller have access. We keep your data while you use VestibuLab or until you delete your account or request its cancellation.

Transfers

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. It is only processed by the technology providers needed to run the app (Google Firebase, Gmail to send verification codes, Google Play and Stripe for payments) or when required by a competent authority.

Your rights (ARCO) and how to exercise them

You can access your data, rectify it, request its cancellation or object to its use, and withdraw your consent. Send your request to audiomedicalmx@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, stating your name and what you are requesting. We will reply within 20 business days at most. If you request cancellation, we will delete your record from the database. You can also delete your account yourself in the app: Settings → Delete account (more information).

Purchases and payments

In the Android app, VestibuLab Pro is bought through Google Play with your Google account. In the web version (iPhone, iPad, Mac and computers) payment is processed by Stripe, Inc. In both cases we do not receive or store your card data: we only receive the payment confirmation, your email, the amount and a transaction identifier. That processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Stripe Privacy Policy.

Purchase conditions and the refund policy are in the Purchase terms.

Other services

Google Fonts: with an internet connection, the app downloads fonts from Google's servers, which may receive your IP address as with any web request. VestibuLab does not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

Minors

VestibuLab is intended for health professionals and students aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Educational use

VestibuLab is a learning tool developed with the support of artificial intelligence models and may make mistakes. It is not a medical device or a clinical diagnostic tool: it must not be used to make decisions about real patients and does not replace clinical evaluation.

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes, we will publish the new version on this same page with its date and announce it in the app.
